# Logbook

Dated entries, in order. Every experiment that moves a number gets one.
Three lines, not an essay.

**Format:**

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — <one-line title>
**Tried:** what I changed
**Measured:** the number, before → after
**Concluded:** what it means, or what I still don't understand
```

Write the "Concluded" line even when the answer is "no idea why." Especially then.
The unexplained results are the ones worth coming back to.
